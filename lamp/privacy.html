Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026  ·  Lamp

Overview

Lamp doesn’t collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking.

En español: Lamp no recopila, guarda ni comparte información personal. No hay cuentas, anuncios ni rastreo. Tus historias guardadas y tus ajustes se quedan solo en tu dispositivo. Las voces naturales de Lamp son generadas por IA.

This Privacy Policy describes how Lamp (the "App"), developed by Japps.ai, handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect / Do Not Collect

Lamp has no sign-up and no analytics. We never see who uses it or which stories they read.

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
  • Stories you save and your settings stay on your own device.

Local Storage / Backend Storage

Lamp uses your browser’s local storage to remember your settings: your language, the stories you saved, your chosen voice, reading speed, text size, and appearance. It also keeps an offline copy of the app, and of the voice recordings you’ve played, on your device so it works without internet.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser’s data for this site or removing Lamp from your home screen. Lamp has no backend server or database.

Third-Party Services

Lamp’s natural voices. Lamp’s built-in natural voices are AI-generated recordings, created ahead of time with OpenAI’s text-to-speech and stored with the app on our own website. Playing them doesn’t send anything about you to OpenAI or anyone else. They are AI-generated, not recordings of a human reader.

Device voices. If you choose one of your phone’s voices instead, Lamp uses the speech voices built into your device or browser. Most of these run entirely on your device. Some browsers also offer online voices; if you choose one of those, the text being read may be sent to that voice provider to create the audio, under the provider’s own privacy policy.

Fonts and hosting. Lamp’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, when the page loads.

Bible text. The passages come from the King James Version and the Reina-Valera 1909, both in the public domain.

Children's Privacy

Lamp is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
