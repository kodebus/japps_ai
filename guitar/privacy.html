Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026  ·  Your First Guitar Lessons

Overview

Your First Guitar Lessons stores your practice progress and custom songs locally on your device. If you sign in, your custom songs can sync across devices using your Claude account. No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

This Privacy Policy describes how Your First Guitar Lessons (the “app”), developed by Japps.ai, handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect / Do Not Collect

Your First Guitar Lessons does not collect your name, email address, location, or any personally identifying information. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

  • No account required to use the app
  • No usage analytics or telemetry are collected
  • No advertising identifiers are used
  • Practice progress and sound preferences are stored only on your device via localStorage
  • Custom songs you create may be synced to your Claude account if you are signed in (optional)

Local Storage / Backend Storage

Your First Guitar Lessons uses your browser’s localStorage to remember your practice checkmarks (7-day plan), your preferred sound mode (normal or backup), and any custom songs you create. This data never leaves your device unless you are signed in to a Claude account.

If you use the app while signed in to Claude, your custom songs may be saved to your Claude account database so they sync across your devices. This sync is handled by Anthropic’s infrastructure under their own privacy policy. No other data is transmitted to any server.

Third-party fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (font files only; no personal data is transmitted). PDF import uses PDF.js, loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com — PDF files are processed entirely within your browser and are never uploaded anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Your First Guitar Lessons uses the following third-party services in a limited, privacy-respecting way:

  • Google Fonts — loads the Poppins and Inter typefaces for display. Only font files are requested; no personal data is sent to Google.
  • PDF.js (via cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — loaded on demand only when you import a PDF into the song editor. PDF processing happens entirely in your browser; the file is never uploaded to any server.

Children’s Privacy

Your First Guitar Lessons is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
