1Meet your strings & tune up

Your guitar has 6 strings. Tap one to hear what it should sound like, then turn the tuning peg until yours matches.

From thickest to thinnest: Eddie Ate Dynamite, Good Bye Eddie.

💡 Easiest way to tune: download GuitarTuna or Fender Tune, pluck each string, and turn the peg until the needle centers. Tune every time you play.

2Find the notes on the fretboard

Music uses 7 letters (A–G) that repeat. Between most letters there's a "sharp" note (♯). The two exceptions: B→C and E→F have nothing between them.

Each fret raises the note by one step. Tap any spot on the fretboard to see and hear its note. The top line is your thinnest string.

Tap a fret…
💡 Press the string just behind the metal fret wire using your fingertip. If it buzzes, press a bit harder or move closer to the fret.

3Learn 4 chords that play hundreds of songs

Numbers in the dots = which finger (1=index, 2=middle, 3=ring, 4=pinky). ○ = open string, ✕ = don't play. Tap a chord to hear it.

💡 Start with E minor (just 2 fingers). Then practice G → C → G → D slowly. Clean switching beats speed every time.

4Pick a song

These songs use only your four chords. Tap a song to load it, press play, and strum along with the highlighted boxes. Tap ➕ Add your own song to add any song.

Add your own song

5Pick the melody:

Tab: each line is a string (top = thinnest). Number = fret to press. 0 = open string. Tap any note to hear it, or press play.

★Your 7-day plan (15 min a day)

Short daily practice beats one long session. Check off each day. Checkmarks are saved in this browser.

💡 Sore fingertips the first week or two are normal. If your wrist or hand hurts, stop and rest.
