Who I help

Therapy tailored to you

Individual and group therapy and psychological support for people facing trauma, addiction, and the challenges that come with them. You don't have to struggle with substances to come in.

Women's trauma therapy

Individual or group therapy for survivors of childhood, sexual, domestic, trafficking, and narcissistic trauma, whether or not substances are part of the story.

Together we break patterns of maladaptive coping and self-loathing, moving toward self-care, self-forgiveness, and self-love.

IndividualGroup

Men's trauma therapy

For men who may rely on substances or addictive behavior to cope with childhood abuse, sexual trauma, loss of a job or status, divorce, or religious trauma.

A safe space to process what happened, and maybe say it out loud for the very first time.

IndividualGroup

First responders, military & veterans

Specialized peer groups where firsthand and secondary trauma can be processed, and healthier ways of coping explored beyond alcohol and substances.

You can also be seen alone, or with partners and family, to address how the job affects your mental health and your home.

Peer groupsIndividualCouples & family

Individual & group therapy

Trauma therapy for anyone, and groups for those who may or may not struggle with substances or addictive behavior. We look at how past events feed addiction and self-loathing.

With new coping skills, an end to isolation, and the courage to ask for help, you'll learn you are no longer alone.

About Kim

Transparent. Fearless. The real deal.

Kim is transparent and fearless in addressing the issues that haunt you and keep you awake at night.

With 25 years in the addiction field and a decade focused on trauma and PTSD, she creates a safe space for you and your peers to unburden yourselves, a reminder that you are not alone and no longer have to suffer.

Trained in EMDR, Kim uses it alongside other evidence-based therapies to challenge the negative core beliefs formed in childhood and reinforced by trauma and substance use, building a healthier belief system through acceptance, self-care, and compassion for yourself and others.
